What data the app stores, and where

The app stores the following information, entirely on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account):

  • Kid profile names you create
  • Word lists, folders, and due dates you create or import
  • Practice/review progress and mastery status for each word
  • Any backup files you choose to export

None of this information is sent to us, to our servers (we don't have any), or to any third party. We have no way to see, access, or recover your data — if you lose your device without a backup, that data is gone, the same as any other information stored only on your phone.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored using Apple's CloudKit service, inside the private iCloud account associated with your own Apple ID. This works the same way as syncing Notes or Photos between your own devices. We do not have access to this data — it is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices.

Camera and photo access

If you use the "Scan a Photo" feature to import a word list, the app asks for permission to access your camera or photo library. Any photo you use for this is processed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition (Vision framework) to read the words on it. The photo itself is never uploaded, stored permanently, or transmitted anywhere — it's used momentarily to extract text, and that's it.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may send you a local reminder about an upcoming due date you set. These reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device — no data about you or your child leaves your phone to make this happen.

Paying for the app

Sight Word Buddy is a one-time paid app — you pay once, through the App Store itself, to download it. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase. Your payment information is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive, see, or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing address. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction; you can review it at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third-party services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind in this app. Nothing about how you or your child use this app is measured, profiled, or shared with any outside company.

Children's privacy

Sight Word Buddy is designed for parents and guardians to use with their children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information directly from children. Kid profile names are chosen by the parent/guardian, stored only locally on-device (and in the parent's own private iCloud, if enabled), and are never transmitted to us or any third party. This app is intended to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design — since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, child or adult, off of the device.

Your data, your control

Since everything is stored locally (and optionally in your own iCloud), you are always in full control:

  • You can delete any kid profile, list, folder, or word at any time within the app
  • You can export a full backup of your data whenever you like, and choose exactly where to save or send it
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device
  • If iCloud sync is enabled, turning it off or signing out of iCloud stops any further syncing

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Since we don't collect contact information from you, we're not able to notify you directly — we'd encourage checking back here occasionally if you have concerns.
